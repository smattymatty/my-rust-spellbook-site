# Seed — the day/night cycle (for a future /grill-with-docs)

Not yet designed. Captured while hot so the grill starts from real intent.

## The pitch

The sky already has stars and a moon. Grow it into a **time-of-day cycle**:
sunrise → **daylight** → **noon** → dusk → **midnight** (moon out) → repeat. Sun
arcs across, clouds drift, then the moon comes out.

It is not decoration — **time of day is a mechanic.** Different upgrades,
passives, stances, and sub-strategies apply (or amplify) at different phases. A
build can be tuned to a phase; a stance might want noon, another midnight.

## Why it's the right idea (Camus)

The three phases map straight onto Camus, which is the game's spine:
- **Noon** — lucidity. The clear-eyed revolt, the absurd faced in full sun.
- **Midnight** — the confrontation with the abyss; the dark night of thought.
- **Daylight** — the labor itself, the ordinary push.

"Midnight thinking, daylight thinking, noon thinking" — the operator's words. A
build that thrives at noon vs one that thrives at midnight is a real, flavorful
fork, and it's *his* philosophy rendered as a mechanic (same move as Stances).

## Open questions for the grill (do not answer yet)

- **Phases:** how many, named what, how long each. Is it a smooth clock or
  discrete phases? Real-time or tied to the loop (e.g. advances per roll-back)?
- **What each phase changes:** flat buffs? unlocks? which upgrades/passives are
  phase-gated? Does a stance have a "home" phase?
- **Player control:** can you influence the time (speed it, hold it), or is it
  ambient and you build around it?
- **Visuals:** sun arc, cloud drift, sunrise/sunset colour shifts, moonrise — and
  how the existing star/moon scene becomes the midnight phase.
- **Flavor via titles, no cringe:** upgrade/passive names carry the flavor; the
  copy stays plain and functional (operator's standing rule).
- **Interaction with everything built:** stances, sub-builds, scorn upgrades,
  defiance trees, universes — where does phase sit in that stack?
